Full-system architectural simulators such as gem5 are powerful but opaque: it is hard to attribute simulated micro-architectural events back to the software that caused them. This work introduces lightweight call-stack profiling for gem5, attributing simulated architectural behavior to source-level call stacks.

By making complex simulator behavior observable and explainable, the technique supports trustworthy architecture simulation---a core requirement for building and validating new modeling frameworks.
