DICE is an open architecture-level simulation framework for detailed inter-chiplet end-to-end PHY-link modeling.

## Repository Contents

DICE models cross-package chiplet communication as a physical-layer-aware process rather than a fixed latency. The framework captures:

- Inter-chiplet PHY-link behavior
- Signal integrity effects
- Forward error correction and retransmission
- Latency variability and stochastic link behavior
- Architecture-level consequences of chiplet communication
